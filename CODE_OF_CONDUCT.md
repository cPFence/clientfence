# Code of Conduct

ClientFence follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). Everyone who takes part in
the project, in issues, pull requests or any other project space, is expected to:

- be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience;
- discuss the work, not the person, and accept that people disagree;
- not harass, insult or threaten anyone, and not publish others' private information;
- not post sexual, violent or hateful content.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by email at support@cpfence.app.
Reports are reviewed promptly, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may warn,
or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who does.
